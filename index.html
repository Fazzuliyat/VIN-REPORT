Where the data comes from

Built on public records, not guesswork

The specs and recall data behind every report come straight from NHTSA — the same U.S. federal registry that manufacturers themselves report to. We don't scrape listings or estimate values; every field either comes back from a live government API call, or the report tells you plainly that we don't have it.

That second part matters. A lot of "free VIN check" sites imply they cover accident and title history when they don't have access to it. We'd rather tell you exactly what's checked than pad the report with guesses.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this the same as Carfax or AutoCheck?

No, and we want to be upfront about that. Carfax and AutoCheck are approved NMVTIS data providers with access to title, accident, and odometer records. We use free, public NHTSA data for specs, recalls, and safety complaints — a real and useful check, but not a full title history unless a history provider is connected. See the "Title & history flags" section of the report.

Why is the first part free?

The decode step uses a fully public government API with no usage cost to us, so there's no reason to charge for it. The paid report bundles that with recall and complaint lookups and a formatted, save-able page.

How fast do I get my report?

Immediately after payment confirms — the report page loads live the moment your PayPal payment is captured.

What if my VIN won't decode?

Double-check for mixed-up characters (no I, O, or Q ever appear in a VIN). If a valid 17-character VIN still won't decode, contact us before paying — see the refund policy for VINs that fail after payment.

Is my payment secure?

Yes — payment is processed entirely by PayPal; we never see or store your card or bank details.
